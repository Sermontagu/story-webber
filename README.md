# Story Webber

A free planner for novel writers. Map your characters, places and secrets as a web, lay your story out chapter by chapter, split chapters into scenes, and chart how each character changes.

**Try it:** add your link here once GitHub Pages is on (it looks like `https://YOURNAME.github.io/story-webber/`).

## What it does
- **Web:** nodes for characters, places, objects or ideas, with connections between them. Add your own node types and colours.
- **Timeline:** see who is in play in each chapter.
- **Arcs:** score each character chapter by chapter and see the shape of their arc.
- **Chapters and scenes:** name them whatever you like (chapters, episodes, beats). Track draft status and word counts.
- **Structures:** start from Three-act, Save the Cat, Hero's journey or Seven-point, or build your own.
- **What-ifs, clues and open questions** to keep track of plot threads.
- Three looks: Noir, Paper and Dusk.

## Your story stays with you
Everything is saved in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use **Setup → Download backup** now and then, and **Load a backup** to move your story to another device.
